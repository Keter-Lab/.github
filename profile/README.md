# Keter Labs

*Building deterministic security for autonomous systems at the execution layer.*

---

## Why We Exist

The industry is racing to build autonomous systems, but securing them through language models and prompt engineering alone is mathematically fragile.  
At Keter Labs, we believe true agent safety must be enforced at the only layer that ultimately matters: **execution**.

---

## About Keter Labs

**Keter Labs (founded 2026)** is a deliberately small deep-tech research outfit.  
We assemble math and people to attack unsolved intelligence problems, with focus areas including:

- Reflexive intelligence  
- Adversarial systems  
- Complex systems

We optimize for first principles, formal reasoning, and hard technical progress over optics.

---

## About Reality Kernel

**Reality Kernel** is our early founding nucleus.

It is where we are developing:

- Deterministic agent boundary models  
- Offline-enforceable control layers  
- Proof-oriented foundations for secure autonomy

Our current emphasis is on **proofs and early code**, not marketing.

If you believe agent boundaries must be deterministic and enforced offline, you are in the right place.
